📋 Case notes

    How to play

    • Place every suspect and the victim on the board: exactly one per row and one per column.
    • Rooms are the colored regions. Big objects (trees, tables...) block a cell; rugs, beds and chairs can be stood on.
    • Every suspect's clues are true. The victim's room holds exactly two people: the victim and the murderer.
    • Case notes are facts about the whole scene. Clues never mention the victim, so find out who shares the victim's room.
    • Pick a suspect, then tap a cell to pencil a note, or press and hold a cell to place them. Hold on their own cell to remove.
    • Right mouse / two fingers rotates the board, wheel / pinch zooms.
    • Keys: 1-9 suspect, N X E tools, Ctrl+Z undo, Esc deselect.

    Clue words

    beside
    next to it (up, down, left, right) and in the same room.
    alone
    nobody else shares their room.
    corner
    a cell touching two perpendicular walls.
    north / south / east / west of
    strictly above / below / right / left, any distance.
    diagonal
    same diagonal line, not the same cell.
    first ... last
    row or column number counted from the north or west.
